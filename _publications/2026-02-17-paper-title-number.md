excerpt: >-
 Motivated by recent work on (re)mixed Eulerian numbers, we study a subfamily of these numbers introduced by Nadeau and Tewari. We establish a combinatorial interpretation by showing that they arise as generating polynomials of permutations with respect to a multivariate statistic involving left-to-right minima, right-to-left minima, descents, and the mixed major index. Our results simultaneously generalize the bi-Stirling-Eulerian polynomials of Carlitz–Scoville and the Stirling-Euler-Mahonian polynomials of Butler.
