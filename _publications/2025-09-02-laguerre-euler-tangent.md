excerpt: >-
  In this paper, we introduce two families of partial Laguerre histories by imposing natural restrictions on the labels of matched north-east and south-east steps. We show that these two families share the same Jacobi continued-fraction expansion. As applications, we derive new combinatorial interpretations of the Euler numbers and the reduced tangent numbers by excluding certain colored east steps. We also introduce a natural $$(p,q)$$-weight on partial Laguerre histories and obtain $$(p,q)$$-analogues of Euler numbers and reduced tangent numbers.
