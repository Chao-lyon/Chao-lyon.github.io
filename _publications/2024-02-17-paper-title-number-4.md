excerpt: >-
  Motivated by recent works on enumeration of Coxeter groups by the parity of descent positions, we prove a formula for the generating function of the vector statistic $$(\operatorname{odes}_G,\operatorname{edes}_G,\operatorname{col}_G,\ell_G)$$ over the group of colored permutations $$G(r,n)$$. This generalizes and unifies several known results over Coxeter groups of type $$A$$ and $$B$$.
