I obtained my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 under the supervision of Professor Jiang Zeng. My research interests lie in enumerative combinatorics and algebraic combinatorics, with a particular focus on permutation statistics, Eulerian polynomials, and related topics.
